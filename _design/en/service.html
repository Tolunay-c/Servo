SCOPE

WHAT HAPPENS
AT EACH STAGE

The following is our standard flow. The scope of each project is defined in writing in the quotation.

01

SITE SURVEY AND MEASUREMENT

We examine the line on site: stroke, mounting points, working distance, vibration, surface and ambient light.

02

MECHANICAL DESIGN AND MANUFACTURING

Body dimensions, mounting interface and supporting structure are designed for the application; manufacturing and assembly are done in our own workshop.

03

CONTROL SOFTWARE AND INTEGRATION

Recipe structure, operator panel and data exchange with line automation are prepared before commissioning.

04

INSTALLATION

The system is mounted on the line, mechanical references are taken and power and data connections are made.

05

COMMISSIONING AND VERIFICATION

Recipes are defined, positions are verified and the system is run with real product.

06

OPERATOR HANDOVER

Panel use, recipe definition and daily checks are practised with the operator and the maintenance team.

AFTERWARDS

MAINTENANCE, SPARE PARTS
AND SUPPORT

Limited maintenance items

In the shuttle architecture all movement is concentrated in one servo axis and the carriages carry no moving electrical connections.

Spare parts

Laser module, carriage and drive components can be replaced individually; the critical spare list is agreed at handover.

Remote support

Where the automation infrastructure allows it, fault diagnosis and recipe support can be provided remotely.

Extension

When the recipe grows, carriages can be added without replacing the body — the reserve area is planned for this from the start.

Warranty period, scope and response conditions vary by project and are defined in writing in the quotation.
