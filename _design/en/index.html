CATEGORY DIFFERENCE

NOT A FIXED SETTING.
POSITIONING BY RECIPE.

Traditional fixed reference

The laser is positioned once during commissioning. When the recipe changes, the operator re-adjusts the line; the result depends on who does it, how it is measured and how much time is available.

Servo-controlled reference

Dimensions are entered on the panel and the system drives to the target positions itself. Every time the same recipe is called, the lines return to the same place — the changeover becomes a machine task instead of an operator task.
