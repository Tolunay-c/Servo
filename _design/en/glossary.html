GLOSSARY

ALIGNMENT
IN PLAIN WORDS

Twenty-four terms used across these pages, written the way they are used on the line — so that a specification can be read without a decoder ring.

TERMS

WHAT THE WORDS MEAN

Where a Turkish term is in common use on site, it is given as an alternative.

Stroke

The real travel range the lines must cover. It is the difference between the narrowest and the widest recipe — not the same thing as the total working width of the machine.

travel range · working width

Recipe

The stored set of position and dimension values for one product. Target positions are defined per product; when the product changes, the recipe is called up.

product setup · stored setting

Projection accuracy

The deviation limit of the projected line against its target position. On ServoMotion systems it is ±1.00 mm. It should not be confused with the cutting tolerance of the product.

±1.00 mm

Working distance

The vertical distance between the laser module and the material surface. It determines line thickness, focus setting and the tolerance on mounting angle.

mounting height

Reference line

The projected line the operator looks at while aligning material or a machine element. It does not cut; it shows a position.

Edge reference

The line showing where the edge of the material has to sit. It is the most common reference type in laying and feeding applications.

Centre / symmetry reference

The position the axis of the material has to meet. In symmetric laying it keeps both edges positioned against a common centre.

Motion configuration

The layout defining how the lasers are positioned on Core Precisor. Six models (K1–K6) cover single/dual channel and fixed/moving laser combinations.

K1 – K6

Channel

One motion zone. In a single channel the lasers move one after another on the same path; in a dual channel two zones move independently.

Shuttle

The single servo axis running on the lower rail of Shuttle Precisor. It carries the laser carriages one by one to their recipe positions and returns to its home position when finished.

Laser carriage

The unit carried on the middle rail holding one laser module. It locks mechanically on the rail once it reaches its position.

Reserve zone

The parking area at the end of the rail where carriages not used by the current recipe wait. They come into service without added hardware when the recipe grows.

Rail length

The sum of stroke, reserve zone and safety margin. Body dimensioning follows from this value.

Setup cycle

The time from calling a recipe until every line has reached its position. In the shuttle architecture it grows with carriage count and stroke, but not linearly.

Repeatability

The ability of the lines to return to the same position when the same recipe is called again. It is the measure of setup no longer depending on the individual operator.

Closed-loop control

Continuous encoder feedback of the servo axis position and comparison against the target. It lets the system correct position error itself.

encoder feedback

Fieldbus

The industrial communication link between line automation and the system. Recipe information and status data travel over it.

saha veri yolu

PLC

The industrial controller running the line. Target positions are passed to the servo drive and status is monitored here.

Servo drive

The power unit driving the servo motor according to speed and position commands. It keeps motion smooth and position accurate.

Line thickness

The thickness of the projected line on the material. Together with working distance and focus it determines readability.

Focus

The optical setting made for the distance at which the laser line appears thinnest. It is reviewed again whenever the working distance changes.

Laser colour

Red or green. The choice follows surface colour, gloss and ambient lighting; on dark or glossy surfaces green can be more readable.

IP protection class

The degree of protection of a component against dust and water. IP54 is a typical value for the laser modules.

Commissioning

The stage after the system is mounted on the line: defining recipes, verifying positions and running with real product.

devreye alma
