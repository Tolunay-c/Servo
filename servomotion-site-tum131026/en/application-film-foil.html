APPLICATION · FILM AND FOIL

EDGE POSITION REFERENCE
IN FILM AND FOIL LINES

In film and foil lines, everything starts with where the two edges will sit. Reel width and cut width change from recipe to recipe, so the edge position has to be re-established at every changeover. ServoMotion moves the two edge lines to the targets read from the recipe, and the positions stay visible while the line runs.

ON THE LINE

DO THESE
SOUND FAMILIAR?

A new edge on every reel change

When the reel width changes, the edge position is measured from scratch, making setup the longest item in the changeover.

Transparent material fools the eye

On a thin, glossy surface, where the edge actually ends depends on the viewing angle. The same recipe is set up differently across shifts, resulting in inconsistent output.

Constant edge scrap

Running with a safety margin adds a little scrap to every metre, and that adds up to a meaningful figure by the end of the year.

Mechanical guides drift

Fixed guides shift position with vibration and need periodic readjustment.

WHAT WE MEASURE ON SITE

PARAMETERS THAT
DEFINE THE SYSTEM

The parameters below drive the design decisions in this segment. We measure these values on your line and base the quotation on that data.

Reel width rangeThe difference between the narrowest and widest recipe determines travel range and housing length.
Edge toleranceThe accepted deviation; line thickness and working distance are selected accordingly.
Material surfaceTransparent, matte or metallised surfaces affect laser colour and optical power.
Working distanceThe vertical distance between the laser and the material determines angular tolerance.
Line speedDetermines how quickly a position change has to complete.
Support structureAn existing traverse or a new frame.
