HOW WE GOT HERE

FROM ONE PRODUCT
TO A SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Product development, application-specific engineering and system design: these stages shaped how the manufacturing experience behind OEM Lazer was carried into ServoMotion.

2014FOUNDED

A FOCUS ON LASER TECHNOLOGY

OEM Lazer was founded to develop and manufacture professional alignment lasers and accessories. Working as a manufacturer focused on laser technology gave us the product knowledge and application experience that everything since has been built on.

01PRODUCT EXPERTISE

FROM A LASER LINE TO APPLICATION KNOWLEDGE

Marking and alignment products taught us how widely working conditions differ. We began to look past the specification of the laser itself and focus on how the line is used on the production surface and what the operator actually needs.

02SOLUTION DEVELOPMENT

TREATING THE PRODUCT AS PART OF THE MACHINE

Application-specific projects widened that view. By taking laser placement, mounting and operating layout together, we moved from supplying a product to developing a solution adapted to the machine.

03SYSTEM APPROACH

FROM A FIXED REFERENCE TO CONTROLLED MOTION

Changing product sizes and setup requirements mean the laser reference has to change with them. Combining laser technology with motion and automation, we developed a system approach in which the positions are managed from the recipe.

04SERVOMOTION

EXPERIENCE TURNED INTO A PRODUCT FAMILY

CORE PRECISOR and SHUTTLE PRECISOR answer different layout requirements through two system architectures. ServoMotion is where our laser expertise meets mechanical design, motion control and application experience.

TODAYCONTINUOUS DEVELOPMENT

ENGINEERING THAT LEARNS FROM THE FIELD

Where that journey stands today is a family of systems adaptable to different requirements rather than a single laser product. We keep developing CORE PRECISOR and SHUTTLE PRECISOR, and we carry what we learn on site into the next design decision.

OUR QUALITY CULTURE

DEVELOPMENT COMPLETED
BY VERIFICATION

The product tests carried out for OEM Lazer in TÜV Rheinland laboratories are one concrete step in that quality journey. That test record supports an approach in which development, verification and documentation are handled together.

We apply the same discipline at ServoMotion. The performance and compliance requirements of each system are assessed within the scope of its own application, and design decisions are weighed against the conditions the system will work in.

WHAT SUCCESS MEANS TO US

TURNING TECHNOLOGY
INTO VALUE

We judge our work by what the product does once it is in a production environment. Three principles shape how we approach it.

UNDERSTAND THE REQUIREMENT

We listen to the application before defining a solution. Production conditions and operating expectations are the starting point for every engineering decision.

DESIGN THE WHOLE

We assess the product as part of the system it will work in, not on its own. Design, control and site commissioning are treated as one shared responsibility.

Experience from each application is carried into the next. Technical support and a system structure that can be extended are what make long-term working relationships possible.
