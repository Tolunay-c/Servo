SHUTTLE PRECISOR

A three-rail architecture for lines that need several projected cutting references visible at the same time. One servo shuttle carries the laser carriages to their recipe positions in sequence. Each carriage locks mechanically in place and stays there when the shuttle returns home.

ARCHITECTURE

THREE RAILS,
ONE MOVING AXIS

Power transmission, carriage guidance and motion drive sit in one rail assembly. One servo axis performs all movement instead of a motor, drive and cable per laser: fewer components and a simpler maintenance routine. The number of servo axes remains unchanged as laser carriages are added, helping limit system complexity as the application scales.

Upper rail — power supply

Lasers are powered continuously at any position along the rail, so moving cables and energy chains are not required.

Middle rail — laser carriages

Each laser travels in its own carriage. When it reaches its position it locks on the rail and stays there independently of the shuttle.

Lower rail — servo shuttle

The only moving axis of the system. It carries the carriages to their recipe positions in sequence and withdraws when finished.

Reserve area

Carriages not used by the current recipe wait in a park zone at the end of the rail and join in as the recipe grows.

SETUP CYCLE

HOW A RECIPE IS BUILT

01

RECIPE

Cut dimensions are entered at the panel, and the system calculates the target position for each laser.

02

APPROACH

The shuttle positions itself under the next laser carriage on the lower rail.

03

TRANSPORT

The carriage is moved to its recipe position with the laser on, so the reference line can be followed as it travels.

04

RELEASE

The carriage is fixed in position and becomes independent of the shuttle.

05

COMPLETION

The shuttle moves to the next carriage; once all lines are set it withdraws to the home position.

System components

Control and motion infrastructure

The laser carriages, the single servo shuttle and the PLC work together to bring every cutting reference to the position held in the recipe. The operator panel gives access to recipe entry and carriage status.

Laser modules

Laser modules on the carriages

  • Red and green laser options
  • One laser module per carriage
  • Focus adjustable to the working distance
  • 12–24 VDC supply

Control system

PLC-based control

  • Carriage target positions calculated from the recipe
  • Setup sequence planned and managed
  • Active and reserve carriages tracked
  • Drive communication over PROFINET
  • Operating status and fault diagnostics
Motion system

Single-axis shuttle drive

  • The only moving servo axis in the system
  • Closed-loop position control with encoder feedback
  • Separate speed profiles for empty approach and loaded transport
  • Synchronous communication over PROFINET
  • The number of axes stays the same as reference lines are added
Mechanical group

Three-rail structure and carriages

  • Separate upper, middle and lower rails
  • A carriage locks mechanically to the rail once it reaches its position
  • Carriages outside the travel range wait in the reserve zone
  • Carriages are added and lasers replaced from the rail
  • Rail assembly length scales with the travel range of the line

SAFETY

LASER SAFETY AND COMPLIANCE

Laser class and labelling

The class of the line laser module used in the system is stated on the product label and in the user manual delivered with it. Modules must be operated under the conditions defined in that manual.

During operation

The beam must not be viewed directly. Reflection angles on glossy surfaces are assessed together during installation, and the operator’s working position is reviewed at commissioning.

During maintenance

The system must be de-energised and the line brought to a safe state before optics cleaning and mechanical checks.

Certificate of conformity

The industrial positioning laser modules used in the system have been assessed against EN 55011 and EN 55032 electromagnetic compatibility requirements and certified by TÜV Rheinland LGA Products GmbH (Cert. No. AK 50498967 0001). Download the certificate (PDF).

Conformity documentation

The declaration of conformity and the technical file for the delivered system are supplied with the delivery package, project by project.
